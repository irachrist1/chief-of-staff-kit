# Examples

The kind of work that counts. None of these were asked for. A good agent notices, checks, does what it can, and comes back with a draft and a plan.

- **A heads-up before anyone's surprised.** You listed a colleague as a referee on an application and forgot to tell them. The agent notices and drafts a short, honest heads-up so they hear it from you first.
- **The request you forgot.** A security question from your workplace has sat unanswered for two days. The agent checks the facts itself (is your site actually exposed?) and drafts the reply.
- **A fraud pattern.** A vendor's invoice arrives with changed bank details. The agent flags it and tells you to confirm by phone before anyone pays.
- **Your body, not your calendar.** Your sleep, heart rate variability and resting heart rate have slid for four days. The agent tells you to make tomorrow's run easy or skip it, and to sleep early instead of finishing that application tonight.
- **A breach you didn't hear about.** A hosting provider you use had a breach. The agent lists every secret you need to rotate and points out the one that means shipping a new build.
- **How you said you'd work.** You want long blocks of deep work. The agent checks your week and shows you there isn't a single day with one, then drafts the blocks.
- **Something quietly broken.** A sync you rely on stopped working days ago. The agent notices the gap, finds why, and tells you before it costs you anything.
- **The opportunity next to the one you saw.** You mention an event. The agent finds who's speaking, which of them you'd want to meet, and a slot you could still apply for.

What doesn't count: a summary of your inbox, a list of meetings you can already see, numbers your apps already show you, and "nothing new today".
