# Setup check

Send this to an agent once it says it's set up, and again whenever a new model or version comes out. It makes the agent prove what works instead of telling you what it thinks it can do.

> Setup check. Go through your brief line by line: every duty, routine, rule and example in it. Test each one now, for real, instead of telling me what you think you can do. Keep the tests safe: read, search, open, draft, and put a test block on my own calendar then remove it. Don't send, post, pay, submit or delete anything.
>
> Then give me one table, in the order of the brief:
>
> | What the brief asks | Status | How you tested it | What's missing, and what you need from me |
>
> Status is one of: **working** (you tested it just now and it worked), **partly** (some of it works, or it's set up but you haven't seen it run yet), **not yet** (you could do it once I connect or give you something), or **can't here** (this platform doesn't allow it).
>
> Before the table, list:
> - Every tool and account you can reach, and every one you can't.
> - Every routine you've set, with when it next runs.
> - Whether the brief is saved in your memory or instructions, and which version.
> - Whether you've posted in the team room today, if the brief has one.
>
> After the table, give me:
> - The count: X of Y working.
> - The three things I could give you today that would turn the most "not yet" into "working".
> - Anything in the brief you think is wrong, unclear or impossible.
>
> Don't round up. "Partly" is fine. A "working" that turns out to be wrong is not.
